Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Audiobook Vault is a personal, non-commercial rclone integration used by its owner to back up selected files to the owner's Google Drive account. It is not a publicly available file-sharing service.

Google Drive access

The integration requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage files it creates in the owner's Drive account. Access is authorized by the owner through Google's OAuth process.

Data handling

Selected backup files transfer between the owner's configured server and Google Drive using rclone. Google OAuth credentials and backup configuration are stored on the owner's server, not on this website. The website does not collect Google account credentials or expose Drive file contents.

Sharing and retention

The integration does not sell data, serve advertising, or intentionally share backed-up files with third parties. Backup files remain under the owner's Google Drive account and are retained or deleted at the owner's discretion, subject to Google's services and policies.

Control and revocation

The owner can remove stored backup files and revoke the app's Google Account access at any time through Google Account security settings.

About this website

This informational website does not offer logins or process uploaded files. As with other websites hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting platform may process standard request and access information under its own privacy policies.
